Paris

Paris was one of those places that felt familiar even on the first visit. Walking through the streets with friends, stopping for random photos, and sitting at small cafés made the experience simple but unforgettable

Landmark

Paris is filled with landmarks that define its identity. The Eiffel Tower stands tall over the city, while the Louvre Museum holds some of the most famous artworks in the world. Walking along the Seine River gives you a calm view of the city from a different angle.

Food

Food in Paris is part of the experience. Fresh croissants in the morning, simple baguette sandwiches during the day, and full meals at night made every day feel complete.

Culture

Street music, art, and fashion are everywhere. You see artists painting in public spaces and performers entertaining small crowds.

Personal Memory

One evening, we sat near the Eiffel Tower as it lit up. Nobody said much. We just watched and took it in. That moment stayed with me more than anything else.

Why We Stayed

Paris taught me to slow down. Not every moment needs a plan. Sometimes, sitting and observing is enough.

Tokyo

Tokyo felt like stepping into the future. Everything moved fast, yet everything worked perfectly. It was one of the most organized cities I have ever seen.

Landmark

Shibuya Crossing is one of the busiest intersections in the world. Tokyo Tower gives you a full view of the city. Every area feels different from the next.

Food

From sushi to ramen, every meal felt fresh and carefully prepared. Even small shops served food that tasted better than expected.

Culture

Tokyo runs on order. People move fast, but everything feels controlled. Trains arrive on time, streets stay clean, and even crowded places feel organized. At the same time, you find quiet areas where everything slows down.

Personal Memory

We entered a small restaurant without knowing what to expect. Nobody spoke much English, but the staff still helped us with a smile. We pointed at the menu, tried something new, and ended up enjoying one of the best meals of the trip.

New York

New York felt loud, busy, and alive at every hour. There was always something happening, no matter where you went.

Landmark

Times Square, Central Park, and the Statue of Liberty are some of the most known spots. Each place offers a different side of the city.

Food

You find food from all over the world. Pizza slices, street food, and small restaurants all add to the experience.

Culture

New York feels like many worlds in one place. You hear different accents, see different styles, and experience different lifestyles in one street. Everyone seems focused on something, moving with purpose.

Personal Memory

We got tired one afternoon and sat on a bench in Central Park. After hours of noise, that quiet space felt different. People walked by, some running, some relaxing, and for a moment, everything slowed down.

What Stayed With Me

New York made me understand energy. The city keeps moving, and you either move with it or step aside and watch.
